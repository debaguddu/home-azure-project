Enterprise Medallion Data Platform Architecture

Metadata-Driven Ingestion, Medallion Processing & Semantic BI Layer
Azure Databricks & ADF

🥉 Bronze Layer (Raw)

Catalog: bronze_catalog.raw_data

As-is ingest from on-premises SQL Server. Technical audit columns (InsertedTimestamp, UpdatedTimestamp, RecordAction) and duplicate primary key validation per landed CSV file.

Policies Customers Claims CoverageTypes Agents

🥈 Silver Layer (Conformed)

Catalog: silver_catalog.insurance_conformed

Standardized, typed, and cleansed dimensional model. Deduplicated customer records, telephone/email formatting, and SCD Type 1 upserts.

dim_customer dim_agent dim_coverage fct_policy fct_claim

🥇 Gold Layer (Analytics)

Catalog: gold_catalog.insurance_analytics

Star schema marts and monthly pre-aggregated analytics. Pre-computes loss ratios, profit margins, turnaround days, and customer risk tiers.

fact_policy_performance fact_claims_settlement agg_agent_performance agg_claims_loss_ratio agg_customer_risk

💎 Semantic Layer (Views)

Catalog: gold_catalog.semantic_reporting

Business-oriented Unity Catalog views optimized for Power BI Direct Lake / SQL Warehouses, C-Suite KPIs, and claims adjudicators.

vw_executive_kpi_dashboard vw_claims_analyst_workbench vw_agent_leaderboard vw_customer_360

p.CustomerID = cust.CustomerID 		Claims adjudication: flags claims below deductible, calculates Payout %, and classifies claims into High/Medium/Low Severity Tiers.
fact_policy_performance agg_agent_performance_monthly AgentID, MonthYear GROUP BY AgentID, MonthYear Agency performance: ranks agents by Written Premium, calculates Agent Loss Ratio, and identifies top producers.
fact_policy_performance agg_claims_loss_ratio_by_coverage CoverageCode, MonthYear GROUP BY CoverageCode, MonthYear Line of Business analysis: tracks product profitability across Auto, Home, Life, and Health coverages over time.
fact_policy_performance agg_customer_risk_profile CustomerID GROUP BY CustomerID Customer 360 intelligence: categorizes customers into Prime (Zero Claims), Standard Risk, or High Risk (Unprofitable).

PL_Master_Metadata_Ingestion Ingestion Orchestrator TriggerName, LoadType, DatabricksJobId Lookup active tables from IngestionMetadata → Parallel ForEach_Table Coordinates Child 1 and Child 2 across all active tables

PL_Child2_Landing_To_Bronze_Raw Raw Delta Processing SourceTableName, FileName, WatermarkColumn Invokes Databricks Job with file_name → Evaluates per-file DQ → Delta MERGE → Updates Watermark bronze_catalog.raw_data.{Table}

PL_Child4_Silver_To_Gold Gold & Semantic Deploy SilverCatalog, GoldCatalog Executes nb_transform_silver_to_gold → Executes nb_create_semantic_views gold_catalog.insurance_analytics & gold_catalog.semantic_reporting
