The Multi-Hop Medallion Architecture Technical Blueprint

🥉 Bronze (Raw Ingestion)

  • Schema: bronze_catalog.raw_data
  • Storage: adls://raw/claims/{Table}/delta
  • Lossless raw ingest preserving source fidelity.
  • Enforces Delta MERGE with Schema Evolution.
  • Automatic audit columns: InsertedTimestamp, UpdatedTimestamp, RecordAction ('I'/'U').
  • Per-file primary key uniqueness validation.

🥈 Silver (Cleaned & Conformed)

  • Schema: silver_catalog.insurance_conformed
  • Standardized telephone, lowercase emails, trimmed strings.
  • Data typing (Decimal for currency, Date for timestamps).
  • Conformed Dimensions: dim_customer, dim_agent, dim_coverage.
  • Conformed Facts: fct_policy, fct_claim with turnaround cycle times.
  • SCD Type 1 automated upserts.

🥇 Gold (Curated Business Marts)

  • Schema: gold_catalog.insurance_analytics
  • Curated Star Schema Marts for underwriting and claims.
  • Pre-aggregates: Loss Ratio %, Net Underwriting Margin, and Claims Severity Tiers.
  • Monthly rollup marts for agency and coverage performance.
  • Customer risk scoring: Prime vs High-Risk Claimant.

💎 Semantic Views (Direct BI)

  • Schema: gold_catalog.semantic_reporting
  • Designed for Power BI Direct Lake & SQL Warehouses.
  • vw_executive_kpi_dashboard: C-Suite KPI summary.
  • vw_claims_analyst_workbench: Adjudicator triage.
  • vw_agent_leaderboard: Producer rankings.
  • vw_customer_360: Single customer view.

Core Business Metrics & Formulae Business Intelligence

1. Loss Ratio Percentage (%)

Loss Ratio = (Total Incurred Claims / Gross Written Premium) * 100

Evaluates underwriting profitability. Health thresholds:

  • ≤ 60%: Profitable (Healthy portfolio)
  • 61% - 75%: Acceptable operational margin
  • > 75%: Critical (Loss incurring)

2. Claims Payout Settlement Ratio

Payout Ratio = (Settled Amount / Claim Amount) * 100

Monitors adjudication variance. Highlights:

  • Claims settled below policy deductible.
  • Anomalous claim amounts exceeding normal baseline.
  • Severity classification (≥ $50K High, ≥ $10K Med).

3. Turnaround SLA & Settlement Days

Settlement Days = DATEDIFF(ClaimDate, IncidentDate)

Measures customer claim adjudication turnaround time:

  • > 30 Days Pending: Urgent SLA Breach
  • 15 - 30 Days: SLA Warning
  • < 14 Days: On Track

4. Customer Lifetime Contribution

Net Contribution = Lifetime Premium - Lifetime Claims Paid

Customer segmentation for retention and cross-sell:

  • Prime: Zero claims filed.
  • High Risk: Payout exceeds lifetime premium.
  • Cross-Sell Target: Single policy with clean history.

Enterprise Security, Governance & Audit Trust & Compliance

Unity Catalog

  • Unified Metastore governing Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Semantic views.
  • Three-level namespace isolation: catalog.schema.table.
  • Centralized data lineage and column-level audit.

Managed Identity (MSI)

  • Zero hardcoded passwords or PAT tokens in pipelines.
  • ADF linked services connect to Databricks and ADLS Gen2 via Azure MSI.
  • Azure Key Vault protects database credentials.

State & Audit Logging

  • Centralized audit log: dbo.PipelineExecutionLog.
  • Tracks BatchID, PipelineRunID, RowsCopied, and Runtimes.
  • Stored procedures for start/end execution logging.

Environment Maintenance Utility

Dedicated service pipeline PL_Service_Cleanup_Tables and notebook nb_service_cleanup_tables allow 1-click test environment resets (dropping Databricks tables, wiping external storage, and resetting watermarks).
